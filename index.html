About Us

We Make Small Spaces Live Large.

Exquisite Space Saver Furniture Limited conceptualizes, fabricates, manufactures and markets unique space-saving furniture. In apartments, homes and offices where every metre matters, our Space-Saving Furniture (SSF) series prevents your rooms from ever feeling cramped.

Our pieces help enlarge space, lower costs, reduce clutter and make your living room or office appear larger and inviting — every design is multifunctional, and we are committed to growing our SSF ideas every single year.

  • Foldable multipurpose, round, square, side & stand-up tables
  • Pop-up centre tables, 3-tier coffee tables & rotating-box tables
  • Wall-mount tables with picture frames & home-office compacts
  • Folding chairs, recliners, pouf ottomans & customized sofas
  • Bespoke wardrobes, HDF doors, kitchen cabinets & office furniture

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Can’t find your answer? Call 0802 307 7953 or message us on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver outside Ikorodu?

Yes. We deliver across Lagos State and can arrange waybill transport to other states. Delivery fees depend on location and order size — message us for a quote.

Can I order a custom size, colour or fabric?

Absolutely. Customization is our specialty — dimensions, wood finishes, upholstery fabrics and door designs are all made to your brief. Share a photo or sketch and we will fabricate it.

How long does a bespoke piece take?

Ready-made foldables are available immediately at the showroom. Bespoke wardrobes, kitchens and doors typically take 1–3 weeks depending on scope and finishing.

Do you offer bulk or institutional discounts?

Yes — schools, churches, event centres and corporate offices enjoy special pricing on volume orders of tables, desks, benches and chairs.

Can I buy online?

Yes, selected products are listed on our Konga store at konga.com/merchant/exquisite-table-and-chairs, or you can order directly via WhatsApp and pay on confirmation.

Is there a warranty on your furniture?

Every piece carries a workmanship guarantee. Hinges, locks and folding mechanisms are serviced free within the warranty period, and we offer affordable repairs for life.
